Remake Ambassador

Duration: February 2024 - May 2025

Remake is a global advocacy organization fighting for fair pay and climate justice in the clothing industry. As a brand ambassador, I function as a fashion activist and educate the public on poverty wages, unsafe conditions and gender-based violence with which our clothes are made today through published articles, research reports, live events, classroom visits, and peer-to-peer engagement. Additionally, ambassadors organize engage brands, unions, press, and policy makers to reshape the industry.
